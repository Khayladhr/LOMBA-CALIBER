2 of 3 correlated parameters are deviating together — this is exactly the pattern that historically preceded coupling failure at this equipment class. A single-parameter check would only flag vibration after it crosses 7.0 mm/s; the composite score already flags it today because flow and vibration are moving together.
Why this matters
Today, these 4 layers live in separate systems — someone has to manually notice the sensor drift, remember the weekly trend, dig through the incident log, then track down the right RCA report. PlantPulse links them automatically: the moment Layer 1 shows abnormal drift, the system already knows what Layer 2–4 say about equipment like this — turning a reactive, hours-long investigation into a same-minute recommendation.
